using old operating systems

as time has progressed, older operating systems have fallen farther and farther into disuse. because of this, they are oftentimes unable to use period accurate external services – and in most cases – most external services at all

i personally think old apple products are best for use in the modern day because they all have some of the best backwards compatibility on earth, beauty, and speed

as an experiment, i daily drove OS X Mavericks for almost 10 months, just to see if the average person could drop into an older operating system and just keep working, and lo and behold, it worked! i was able to browse the web, send messages, program, and game all from a 13+ year old operating system!

as a resource, i used Mavericks Forever where by following the instructions, i was able to fix ssl, browse the web and more, all with a set of easily installable pkg files

on the mobile side of things, a lot of iOS =< 6's broken behavior is fixable by cherry picking from r/LegacyJailbreak including, again, fixed ssl, a whole lot of browsers, and a huge repository of apps.
